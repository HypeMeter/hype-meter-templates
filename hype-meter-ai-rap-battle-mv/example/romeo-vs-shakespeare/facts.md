# Facts used in the lyrics and cards

- "Wherefore art thou Romeo?" means "why are you Romeo", not "where are you". Romeo and Juliet, Act 2, Scene 2.
- The earliest recorded use of the word "lonely" is in Shakespeare's Coriolanus (Oxford English Dictionary).
- Romeo drinks poison in Act 5, about four days after he meets Juliet. Romeo and Juliet.
- "Exit, pursued by a bear" is a real stage direction. The Winter's Tale, Act 3, Scene 3.
- "All the world's a stage" is from As You Like It, Act 2, Scene 7.
