# 10 play page prompts

Paste one into Claude (Claude Code or the Code tab of the desktop app, with this skill installed), or into any chat and add "follow the 6 parts: the thing, the controls, the look inside, the numbers, the words, the one-line idea". Swap the question for your own.

## Science
1. **Why does the Moon always show us the same face?** Draw Earth, the Moon's orbit and a flag on the Moon. Sliders: days (0 to 27.3) and how many times the Moon spins per trip around Earth. One-line idea: it does spin, exactly once per orbit.
2. **Why is it hotter in summer if Earth is farther from the Sun in July?** Draw sunlight hitting the ground at an angle. Sliders: month and latitude. Show how much ground the same beam covers. One-line idea: the angle matters more than the distance.
3. **How does a microwave heat food from the inside out (or does it)?** Draw a bowl with a few layers. Sliders: power and minutes. Show where the heat goes first. One-line idea: microwaves heat the outer few centimetres, then heat spreads inward.

## Money
4. **How much does a leaked API key cost me, with and without a spending cap?** Draw a key and a jar of money. Sliders: hours until I notice, spend per hour; a select for the cap. One-line idea: a cap decides the most a leak can cost.
5. **Why does paying a bit more each month shorten a loan so much?** Draw the balance as a bar going down. Sliders: extra payment and interest rate. Show months saved and interest saved. One-line idea: extra money goes straight to the part that grows.
6. **How does a small monthly subscription add up?** Draw a stack of coins per month. Sliders: price and months; a select for how many subscriptions. One-line idea: you don't feel it per month, you feel it per year.

## AI and work
7. **Why does an AI reviewer find something new every time?** Draw bars per review round: real problems vs nitpicks. Sliders: round and nitpicks per round; a select for catch rate. One-line idea: real problems shrink, nitpicks don't, so decide when to stop.
8. **Why do longer chats with an AI get slower and more expensive?** Draw a message that has to look back at every earlier message. Slider: chat length. Show the cost growing faster than the length. One-line idea: every new word looks back at all the old ones.
9. **Does running 5 AI tasks at once make me 5 times faster?** Draw the AI tasks and one person checking their work. Sliders: number of tasks, minutes the AI needs per task, minutes I need to check each one. Rule: all tasks run at the same time, then I check them one by one. Show the finished tasks piling up while they wait for me. One-line idea: the part you have to do yourself sets the speed limit.

## Everyday objects
10. **How does a fridge move heat out of the box?** Draw the box, the pipes on the back, and the liquid inside. Step buttons: squeeze, cool down, expand, soak up heat. One-line idea: it doesn't make cold, it carries heat outside.
