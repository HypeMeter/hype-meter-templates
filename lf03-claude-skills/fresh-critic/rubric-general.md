# Rubric: anything you're about to send or publish

1. **Is anything wrong?** Facts, dates, weekdays, names, numbers, prices, links.
2. **Is anything missing?** What will the reader ask right after reading? What happens if the plan changes?
3. **Is any request unclear?** Who needs to do what, by when (with a date, not "Monday").
4. **Is the tone right?** For this reader, in this situation.
5. **Anything made up?** A detail that sounds plausible but wasn't in the source.
6. **Anything private?** Keys, passwords, personal details, internal names that shouldn't go out.
