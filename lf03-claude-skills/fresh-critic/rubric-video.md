# Rubric: video script, title, thumbnail text, description

Check in this order. Most important first.

1. **Is every claim true and checkable?** Numbers, dates, "I tested", "X times faster". Flag anything that sounds bigger than what the script shows, and anything with no source.
2. **First 15 seconds.** Does it say the viewer's problem and what they'll get from watching? Would a stranger keep watching?
3. **Listen-only test.** Read it as if your eyes were closed. Any sentence that's too long, a number that's hard to hear, or a word only insiders know?
4. **Does the viewer leave with something to do today?** A concrete step, a template, a setting. Not just "it depends".
5. **Title.** Starts with the words a viewer would search. Says what they get. Any number has a unit (minutes, files, tests). No riddles, no wordplay. Under 70 characters if possible.
6. **Thumbnail text.** 2–4 words a stranger understands in one second on a phone. Not the same words as the title.
7. **Description.** First line says what the video is. Links work. Says if the voice is AI. No private info.
8. **Honesty.** Are the limits said out loud (small sample, my own scoring, one test)? Does the conclusion match the evidence?
