# 8 HTML video prompts

Paste one into Claude (Claude Code, or the Code tab of the Claude desktop app, with this skill installed). Swap the topic for your own. With the skill installed, Claude shows you the shot list before it builds anything.

1. **Kinetic title.** Make a 6-second vertical video. The words "Nobody reads the second email" appear one by one, big, the last word in yellow. The first frame already shows the first word. Show me the shot list first.
2. **Animated chart.** Make a 10-second wide video of this data as a bar chart that grows in, then highlights the biggest bar with one sentence underneath: [paste your numbers]. Label the source in small text at the bottom: [where the numbers come from].
3. **Before / after.** Make an 8-second vertical video that shows "before" on the left half and "after" on the right half, sliding in one after the other: before = [old way], after = [new way]. End on a frame with both sides and one line that says what changed.
4. **Three steps.** Make a 12-second vertical video that explains [a process] in 3 numbered steps. One step on screen at a time, each with a simple drawn icon (shapes only), then all 3 together at the end.
5. **Quote card.** Make a 7-second vertical video of this quote, revealed line by line, with the speaker's name and the date underneath: "[quote]" - [name], [date]. Only use the exact words.
6. **Countdown.** Make a 10-second vertical video counting down "3 things I'd check before [doing X]", number 3 first, each with a big number and one short line.
7. **Photo slideshow.** Make a 15-second wide video from these photos in this folder: [file names]. Each photo slowly zooms in a little (no more than 1.1x) with a one-line caption. Crossfade between photos.
8. **Explain one number.** Make an 8-second vertical video that makes "[a number, e.g. 2 million seconds is 23 days]" feel real: show the number, then a picture of what it means (dots, a bar, a calendar), then one sentence.

After the first render, useful follow-ups:
- "Make the first frame stronger. It's what people see before they tap."
- "The text in shot 2 is on screen too short. Give it 1 more second."
- "Render just 3 to 6 seconds so I can check the fix."
- "Here's my voice-over (voice.mp3). Time the shots to it and render with it."
