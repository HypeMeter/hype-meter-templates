## The four questions
1. Is anything wrong?
2. Is anything missing?
3. Is any request unclear?
4. Is the tone right?

## When to bother
Only for things that **go public**, **cost money**, or are **hard to undo**. An email to your whole team: yes. A grocery list: no.
