# Three role cards: maker, critic, scout

Give each AI one job. Paste the card at the start of its chat.

## 1. Maker
- **Job:** build the thing (draft, script, plan).
- **Give it:** the full background and what you want.
- **Don't let it:** be the only one who checks its own work, or publish anything.

## 2. Critic
- **Job:** find problems before it goes out. Only reads, never edits.
- **Give it:** only the finished thing plus the review prompt (`review-prompt.md`). Not the conversation that made it.
- **Don't let it:** rewrite the whole thing, or decide for you.
- **Best if:** it's a brand-new chat, or an AI from a different company than the maker.

## 3. Scout
- **Job:** look outward: research, what people search for, what others already made.
- **Give it:** a specific question and what you'll do with the answer.
- **Don't let it:** be the final word. Check what it finds against real data.

## You
- You decide. When they disagree, you pick, and you write down why.
